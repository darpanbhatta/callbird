CallBird

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

CallBird is an iPhone app with an AI assistant that answers the calls you miss or decline, talks with the caller, and sends you a summary. With CallBird Business, the assistant can also answer as your business: it books appointments into a calendar in the app or takes pickup orders. This policy explains what information CallBird handles, why, who it's shared with, and how you can delete it. In this policy, "CallBird", "we" and "us" mean the CallBird team that runs the app.

The short version: we use your information only to run CallBird for you. Your calls and contacts are visible only to you. We don't sell your information, don't use it for advertising, and you can delete it at any time.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in with a text code (no password).
  • Your assistant number, the phone number we set up for your assistant after you subscribe.
  • Your assistant settings: the name callers hear, the voice and personality you pick, greetings, notes you give your assistant, and numbers you block.

Calls your assistant answers

  • Call details: the caller's phone number, the time and the length of the call.
  • Transcripts and summaries: the words spoken on the call are turned into text so the assistant can respond, and we save the transcript and a short summary (who called, why, how urgent, any callback number, and whether the call looks like a scam) so you can read them in the app.
  • Audio is processed live so your assistant can hear and speak. We don't record or keep call audio. The one exception: if our server can't answer a call, the caller can leave a voicemail, which our phone provider (Twilio) records and we turn into text for you.

Contacts (only if you turn it on)

The app can show your contacts' names next to calls. That matching happens on your iPhone. If you turn on greeting by name, the names and numbers of your contacts are sent to our server so your assistant can greet people you know by name. You can turn this off at any time, which deletes them from our server.

CallBird Business (only if you use it)

If you turn on business mode, we also keep:

  • Your business details: the business name, address, opening hours, days closed, services and prices or your menu, booking rules, and the information you give your assistant to answer questions. If you take a photo of your menu, it's sent to our AI provider to read the items and prices; we keep the items, not the photo.
  • Your customers' bookings, orders and waitlist: the name and phone number your customers give, the service or items, the time, any notes, and the status. They come from calls your assistant takes or that you add yourself.
  • Customer history: past bookings and orders are matched by phone number so your assistant can recognize returning customers (it asks to confirm who's calling before using a name) and so you can see a customer list and your monthly stats.
  • Time you block off on your calendar, with the reason you type.

Your assistant takes pickup orders that are paid at pickup. It never asks for or takes card numbers or other payment details on the phone.

Calendar sync: if you choose to show your bookings in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or another calendar app, we create a private link to a calendar feed of your bookings (customer names, phone numbers and notes) and blocked time. Your calendar provider fetches it from that link, and their own privacy policy covers what they do with it. Anyone who has the link can see the feed, so keep it private. You can make a new link in the app at any time, which stops the old one from working.

Subscription and purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or Apple account details. To check whether your subscription is active and which minute packs you've bought, the app uses RevenueCat with a random ID that isn't your phone number or name.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a device token, which we store on your account so we can alert you when a call ends, and, with CallBird Business, about new bookings, orders, waitlist openings, a morning summary of your day, and when your minutes run low. Signing out removes it.

What we don't collect

No advertising IDs, no location, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use it

  • To answer calls on your behalf, talk with callers, and summarize the calls for you.
  • With CallBird Business: to tell callers your free times or menu, book appointments and take pickup orders, keep your calendar, waitlist and customer list, recognize returning customers, and show you what your assistant did.
  • To spot likely scam and spam calls and block numbers you choose.
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure, and enforce usage limits (monthly AI minutes and minute packs).
  • To check your subscription and send you call notifications.
  • To respond when you contact support.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the service. If CallBird is ever involved in a merger or sale, your information would stay under this policy. We never sell your information.

Other users

Each CallBird account is separate. Other users can't see your calls, transcripts, contacts or settings.

A note about callers and customers

People who call you talk with an AI assistant, and the assistant tells them so at the start of the call. What they say is transcribed and summarized for you. Callers can hang up at any time. If you're a caller and want something you said deleted, contact the CallBird user you called, or email us.

If you called a business that uses CallBird Business, the business decides what to do with your booking or order and is responsible for the information it keeps about its customers. We keep that information only to run CallBird for that business. To ask for your details to be changed or deleted, contact the business, or email us and we'll pass your request on.

If you use CallBird Business, you're responsible for having a lawful reason to keep your customers' information and for answering their requests about it, for example by deleting a booking in the app.

How long we keep it

  • Calls stay until you delete them. You can delete any call in the app.
  • CallBird Business: bookings, orders and blocked time are deleted automatically about 4 months after their date, and waitlist entries after about 6 weeks. You can cancel or remove them sooner in the app. Turning business mode off stops new bookings and orders; your business details stay so you can turn it back on, until you delete your account (or email us to delete them).
  • Delete account (Settings in the app) deletes your account, calls, contacts, settings and all business information (bookings, orders, waitlist, customers, menu and calendar link) right away and releases your assistant number.
  • If your subscription ends, your assistant number is kept for 90 days in case you come back, then released. Your calls, contacts and settings are deleted 30 days after that.
  • To prevent abuse of free minutes and phone numbers, after you delete your account we keep a small record of your phone number, the assistant number it had, when it got that number, the minutes you used, and the minute packs you bought (so you don't lose minutes you paid for if you come back). It holds nothing else: no calls, contacts, settings or business information.
  • Service providers may keep limited records (such as call logs or billing records) for the time their own legal and security obligations require.

Your choices and rights

  • See your calls and settings in the app at any time.
  • Delete single calls, your shared contacts, or your whole account in the app. With CallBird Business you can also cancel bookings and orders, remove people from the waitlist, remove blocked time, and make a new calendar link.
  • Turn off notifications, greeting by name, business mode or the morning summary at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information. Email us and we'll help within 30 days.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in sessions are stored as one-way hashes, and each account's data is kept separate. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

Children

CallBird isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app.
