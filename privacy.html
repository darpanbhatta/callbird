CallBird

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

CallBird is an iPhone app with an AI assistant that answers the calls you miss or decline, talks with the caller, and sends you a summary. This policy explains what information CallBird handles, why, who it's shared with, and how you can delete it. In this policy, "CallBird", "we" and "us" mean the CallBird team that runs the app.

The short version: we use your information only to run CallBird for you. Your calls and contacts are visible only to you. We don't sell your information, don't use it for advertising, and you can delete it at any time.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in with a text code (no password).
  • Your assistant number, the phone number we set up for your assistant after you subscribe.
  • Your assistant settings: the name callers hear, the voice and personality you pick, greetings, notes you give your assistant, and numbers you block.

Calls your assistant answers

  • Call details: the caller's phone number, the time and the length of the call.
  • Transcripts and summaries: the words spoken on the call are turned into text so the assistant can respond, and we save the transcript and a short summary (who called, why, how urgent, any callback number, and whether the call looks like a scam) so you can read them in the app.
  • Audio is processed live so your assistant can hear and speak. We don't record or keep call audio. The one exception: if our server can't answer a call, the caller can leave a voicemail, which our phone provider (Twilio) records and we turn into text for you.

Contacts (only if you turn it on)

The app can show your contacts' names next to calls. That matching happens on your iPhone. If you turn on greeting by name, the names and numbers of your contacts are sent to our server so your assistant can greet people you know by name. You can turn this off at any time, which deletes them from our server.

Subscription

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or Apple account details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat with a random ID that isn't your phone number or name.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a device token, which we store on your account so we can alert you when a call ends. Signing out removes it.

What we don't collect

No advertising IDs, no location, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use it

  • To answer calls on your behalf, talk with callers, and summarize the calls for you.
  • To spot likely scam and spam calls and block numbers you choose.
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure, and enforce usage limits (such as monthly AI minutes).
  • To check your subscription and send you call notifications.
  • To respond when you contact support.

We share information only with the service providers that make CallBird work, and only what each one needs. They process it on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the service. If CallBird is ever involved in a merger or sale, your information would stay under this policy. We never sell your information.

Other users

Each CallBird account is separate. Other users can't see your calls, transcripts, contacts or settings.

A note about callers

People who call you talk with an AI assistant, and the assistant tells them so at the start of the call. What they say is transcribed and summarized for you. Callers can hang up at any time. If you're a caller and want something you said deleted, contact the CallBird user you called, or email us.

How long we keep it

  • Calls stay until you delete them. You can delete any call in the app.
  • Delete account (Settings in the app) deletes your account, calls, contacts and settings right away and releases your assistant number.
  • If your subscription ends, your assistant number is kept for 90 days in case you come back, then released. Your calls, contacts and settings are deleted 30 days after that.
  • To prevent abuse of free minutes and phone numbers, after you delete your account we keep a small record of your phone number, the assistant number it had, when it got that number, and the minutes you used that month. It holds nothing else: no calls, contacts or settings.
  • Service providers may keep limited records (such as call logs or billing records) for the time their own legal and security obligations require.

Your choices and rights

  • See your calls and settings in the app at any time.
  • Delete single calls, your shared contacts, or your whole account in the app.
  • Turn off notifications or greeting by name at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information. Email us and we'll help within 30 days.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in sessions are stored as one-way hashes, and each account's data is kept separate. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

Children

CallBird isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app.
