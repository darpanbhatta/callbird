Common questions

How do I set up CallBird?

Sign in with your mobile number, subscribe, and CallBird gives your assistant its own phone number. Then follow the Setup tab to forward the calls you miss or decline to that number. It takes about a minute.

Calls aren't reaching my assistant

  • Open the Setup tab and run the forwarding steps again. Forwarding codes differ by carrier.
  • Make sure the number you're forwarding to is the assistant number shown in Settings.
  • Check you still have minutes left this month (shown at the top of Calls). When you run out, callers can still leave a voicemail.

How do I stop forwarding calls?

On most carriers, dial ##004# and press call to turn off forwarding. Verizon customers dial *73. Do this before deleting your account or canceling.

How many minutes do I get?

CallBird Pro is $9.99 a month and includes 60 minutes of AI-answered calls. CallBird Business is $49.99 a month (or $499.99 a year) and includes 300 minutes. Monthly minutes reset on the 1st.

Can I buy more minutes?

Yes. In CallBird go to Settings → Buy more minutes: 100 minutes for $14.99 or 500 for $59.99. Extra minutes never expire and are used only after your monthly minutes run out. You'll get a notification when you're running low.

CallBird Business

What is CallBird Business?

Your assistant answers as your business. For appointment businesses (salons, clinics, tutors, repair shops and more) it offers only your free times, books, moves and cancels appointments, and keeps a waitlist. For restaurants it knows your menu and prices and takes pickup orders, paid at pickup. Everything shows up in the app right away.

How do I set it up?

Go to Settings → CallBird Business, upgrade, then add your business name, opening hours, and your services or menu (you can take a photo of your menu). Turn on Answer as my business and save.

How do I stop it booking a certain time?

In the Bookings tab tap + and choose Block off time for lunch, an appointment or a day off. Your assistant won't book anyone during that time. Tap the blocked time to remove it.

The kitchen is slammed. Can I slow down orders?

At the top of the Orders tab, choose +15 min or +30 min. Callers hear the longer wait, and it switches off by itself at midnight. Use Sold out to stop offering an item, or Pause orders to stop taking phone orders.

Can I see bookings in Google or Apple Calendar?

Yes. In Bookings tap … → Show in Apple or Google Calendar. Keep the link private; anyone with it can see your bookings. You can make a new link there at any time.

Does the assistant take payments?

No. Orders are paid at pickup, and your assistant never asks for card numbers on the phone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CallBird, or in CallBird tap Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if I cancel?

Your assistant number is kept for 90 days in case you come back, then released. Your calls and settings are deleted 30 days after that.

I'm not getting notifications

In CallBird go to Settings → Notifications and choose Every call. If it says to turn them on in iPhone Settings, allow notifications for CallBird there.

How do I delete my account?

In CallBird go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your calls, contacts and settings right away and releases your assistant number. Turn off call forwarding first (dial *73 on Verizon, or ##004# on AT&T, T-Mobile and most others).

Is my information private?

Your calls and contacts are visible only to you, and we never sell your information. Read the full Privacy Policy.
