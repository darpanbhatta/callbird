Common questions

How do I set up CallBird?

Sign in with your mobile number, subscribe, and CallBird gives your assistant its own phone number. Then follow the Setup tab to forward the calls you miss or decline to that number. It takes about a minute.

Calls aren't reaching my assistant

  • Open the Setup tab and run the forwarding steps again. Forwarding codes differ by carrier.
  • Make sure the number you're forwarding to is the assistant number shown in Settings.
  • Check you still have minutes left this month (shown at the top of Calls). When you run out, callers can still leave a voicemail.

How do I stop forwarding calls?

On most carriers, dial ##004# and press call to turn off forwarding. Verizon customers dial *73. Do this before deleting your account or canceling.

How many minutes do I get?

CallBird Pro is $9.99 a month and includes 60 minutes of AI-answered calls. Minutes reset on the 1st of each month.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CallBird, or in CallBird tap Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if I cancel?

Your assistant number is kept for 90 days in case you come back, then released. Your calls and settings are deleted 30 days after that.

I'm not getting notifications

In CallBird go to Settings → Notifications and choose Every call. If it says to turn them on in iPhone Settings, allow notifications for CallBird there.

How do I delete my account?

In CallBird go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your calls, contacts and settings right away. Your assistant number is held for 90 days in case you come back, then released. Turn off call forwarding first (dial *73 on Verizon, or ##004# on AT&T, T-Mobile and most others).

Is my information private?

Your calls and contacts are visible only to you, and we never sell your information. Read the full Privacy Policy.
